Passkeys

The strongest option: your fingerprint, face, screen lock or a security key. A passkey can’t be phished.

    This browser can’t create passkeys. Try a current version of Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox — or set up an authenticator app below.

    Authenticator app

    An app on your phone that shows a new 6-digit code every 30 seconds — 1Password, Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator and others all work.

    1. In your authenticator app, add an account and scan this code.
    2. Can’t scan it? Type this key into the app instead.
    3. Enter the 6-digit code the app shows.

    Backup codes

    One-time codes for when your phone or key isn’t with you. Each works once, in the same box as an authenticator code.

    Save your backup codes now
