### Classifier v1 (rules tuned on blind_dev errors only)
Hypotheses applied (one commit-sized change each): indirect opt-out phrasing ("rather not get texts"),
fuzzy/no-space keywords ("unsubscibe", "R E M O V E M E"), PT/DE/ZH opt-out phrases, broader `stop`
exclusions (visit/negated/non-contact senses), wrong-number "no one named X", interested service/scheduling
questions, "later" time references, weighted interested-vs-declined resolution, iMessage tapbacks.

| set | accuracy | review rate | opt-outs stopped | unsafe misses | decisive-rule precision |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| blind_dev (tuned on)  | 0.980 | 0.111 | 40/40 | 0 | 0.993 |
| blind_test (held out) | 0.752 | 0.255 | 32/33 | 1 | 0.833 |

**Finding: the rule layer overfits.** +26 points on the tuning set, +4 on held-out. Decisive-rule precision
drops from 0.99 to 0.83 on unseen phrasing, and 5 held-out `interested` replies were predicted `opt_out`
(they would be suppressed — lost revenue, not a compliance risk). Hand-written rules plateau; the
long tail needs the LLM layer or a human. Response: generate a second, broader blind set (dev2 for
inspection, test2 never inspected) rather than looking at blind_test errors.
